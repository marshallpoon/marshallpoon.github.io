// 01 — About

Engineer. Researcher.
Builder.

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer with a Master's from the University of Melbourne and a research semester at ETH Zürich, where I worked on SLAM optimisation and reinforcement learning for robotic locomotion.

My engineering background spans the full stack — from embedded C++ firmware and real-time control systems through to ROS2 simulation environments, CI/CD pipelines, and machine learning integration in robotic systems.

I've worked across space robotics in Melbourne, aerospace hardware in Berlin, computer vision research in Zürich, and energy systems at Tesla. The common thread is a preference for technically demanding, high-accountability work where the engineering decisions are real.

Australian citizen. Eligible for NV2 security clearance. Open to defence, robotics, and space opportunities globally.
