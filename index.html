About

Mechatronics Engineer

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer with a Master's from the University of Melbourne and a research semester at ETH Zürich, where I worked on SLAM optimisation and reinforcement learning for robotic locomotion.

My engineering background spans the full stack — from embedded C++ firmware and real-time control systems through to ROS2 simulation environments, CI/CD pipelines, and machine learning integration in robotic systems.

I've worked across space robotics in Melbourne, aerospace hardware in Berlin, computer vision research in Zürich, and energy systems at Tesla. The common thread is a preference for technically demanding, high-accountability work where the engineering decisions are real.

SLAM · Computer Vision · ETH Zürich

Research Project

Real-time Deployment of WildGS-SLAM

WildGS-SLAM is a monocular RGB SLAM system designed for dynamic environments using uncertainty-aware mapping and Gaussian Splatting techniques. This project focused on profiling bottlenecks, restructuring the pipeline, and optimising runtime performance to achieve real-time deployment.

Robotics · RL · MuJoCo · ETH Zürich

Research Project

Reinforcement Learning for Hexapod & Quadrupedal Locomotion

Trained multi-legged robots to traverse flat, inclined, and uneven terrain using reinforcement learning in MuJoCo simulation. Policy optimisation was framed as a constrained nonlinear problem — reward shaping, cost function design, and gain tuning were the primary levers for achieving stable, adaptive gait patterns including trotting, bounding, and dynamic jumps.
